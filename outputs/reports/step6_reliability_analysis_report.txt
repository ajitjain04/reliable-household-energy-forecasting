13. SUPPORTED FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Based on the audited experimental results, the following findings are
  supported by the measured data:

  F1. Under the tested 30% MCAR missingness protocol, electricity-history
      missingness produced substantially larger performance degradation
      than weather missingness across all four models.

  F2. Under the tested 20% additive Gaussian noise protocol, all four
      models exhibited comparatively small performance changes (< 0.6%
      relative MAE degradation), suggesting that the LSTM architectures
      are relatively insensitive to this level and type of noise.

  F3. Clean predictive accuracy and degradation robustness were not
      perfectly aligned. Early Fusion had the best clean MAE but showed
      larger relative degradation under missingness than Hybrid Fusion.

  F4. The model ranking by absolute MAE was preserved across all tested
      conditions: Early Fusion remained the best-performing model even
      under the most severe degradation (A-A missingness).

  F5. Missing observations were a more disruptive form of degradation
      than additive zero-mean noise under the tested protocols.

14. LIMITATIONS OF THE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Single household dataset only
  - Single test partition (587 windows from Dec 2023 - Jan 2024)
  - One missingness level tested: 30% MCAR
  - One noise level tested: 20% AWGN (eta = 0.20)
  - MCAR missingness only (not MAR or MNAR)
  - White Gaussian noise only (no temporal correlation, no drift, no bias)
  - Frozen-model evaluation only (no retraining under degradation)
  - No formal statistical significance tests performed
  - No natural weather failure events or real sensor-fault labels
  - Wind sin/cos noise may slightly violate the unit-circle constraint
  - Robustness conclusions are conditional on the tested degradation protocols
  - Different missingness/noise levels may produce different rankings
  - Causal mechanisms for observed robustness differences are not established
